About Me

Hi! I'm Connor, an experienced online English and ESL teacher dedicated to helping students reach their language proficiency goals.

My approach is rooted in personalization and real-world application. I believe that learning a new language should be engaging, relevant, and structured to build confidence in speaking, writing, and comprehension.

Whether you are looking to improve your business English, prepare for an exam, or simply become a more fluent speaker, I provide the resources and professional guidance to help you succeed.
