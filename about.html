About Me

Welcome! My name is Connor Reed Brown, and I am an English teacher dedicated to helping students achieve their language goals through personalized, private instruction.

Why Learn With Me?

Expertise

With 3+ years of teaching experience, I have developed effective methods to simplify complex grammar and improve conversational fluency.

Exam Preparation

I specialize in Cambridge exam preparation (PET, FCE, CAE), providing targeted strategies to help you succeed.

Professionalism

As an independent contractor, I am highly organized, punctual, and committed to managing our lessons with professional efficiency.

Personalized Approach

I tailor my programs to your specific needs, whether you are a teen looking for school support or an adult preparing for travel or professional certification.

My Teaching Background

My journey in education began with in-person instruction across Europe before I transitioned to specializing in private online lessons. Over the years, I have had the pleasure of working with a diverse range of students:

  • All Ages: Experience teaching students from kindergarten through sixth grade, as well as teenagers and adults.
  • Diverse Backgrounds: Proven success in helping learners with various native accents and different English proficiency levels, from beginner to advanced.

Why My Classes?

I am originally from California, which means my lessons focus on American English. If your goal is to master the vocabulary, pronunciation, and idiomatic expressions used in the United States, I provide the specialized guidance you need to sound more natural and confident.
